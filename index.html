Family Secretary

Privacy policy. Last updated September 29, 2026.

Family Secretary is a private app that Michael built for his own household. It is not offered to the public. It runs on a computer in our home and is used only by members of our family.

What the app does

It transcribes voice recordings, summarizes them, and suggests to-dos and appointments. With permission from a family member's Google account, it also sorts that person's Gmail by priority, finds possible appointments in email, saves reply drafts to Gmail for the person to review and send themselves, and, only after the person approves, adds events to their Google Calendar and tasks to Google Tasks.

Google user data

  • Gmail: the app reads messages in the inbox so it can sort them and find appointments, adds its own labels, and saves drafts. It never sends email. Sending is always done by the person, in Gmail.
  • Google Calendar: the app reads events to check for conflicts and to list the day's plans, and adds or removes events only when the person approves or undoes one.
  • Google Tasks: the app adds, checks off, or removes tasks only when the person asks it to.

Google user data is used only to provide these features to the person it belongs to. It is processed on our own computer by software that runs there, including the AI model, which runs locally. It is not sold, not used for advertising, not used to train AI models, and not shared with anyone else.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What is stored, and for how long

  • Audio recordings are not kept. Audio is deleted as soon as it has been transcribed. A recording that could not be transcribed is kept for at most 30 minutes so it can be retried, then deleted.
  • Transcripts, summaries, and to-dos are stored on our home computer until the person deletes them.
  • Copies of sorted email are stored on our home computer for 30 days, then deleted.
  • Google sign-in information is stored on our home computer and can be removed at any time.

Notifications

Phone notifications are delivered through the ntfy.sh notification service, so the text of a notification (for example, an email's sender and subject, or an appointment's title and time) passes through that service. The app can be set to send notifications without any details.

Removing access

Anyone who has connected their Google account can remove the app's access at any time from their Google Account, under Security, Third-party apps and services. They can also ask Michael to delete their data from the home computer.
